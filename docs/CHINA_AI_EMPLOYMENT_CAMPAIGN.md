## Work authorisation and relocation checklist

Before accepting an offer, ask the employer's HR or foreign-talent team to confirm in writing:

- Whether the employing entity can sponsor a foreign employee for the relevant work authorisation.
- Whether the role and candidate meet the applicable work-permit criteria.
- Which documents are required and whether qualification or experience evidence needs legalisation or translation.
- Who prepares the work-permit notification and supports the Z-visa application.
- Whether the employer pays visa, permit and relocation costs.
- Whether temporary accommodation, a housing allowance, flights and medical insurance are included.
- Whether the contract is directly with the China employing entity.
- Whether the role requires Mandarin, and the actual working language of the team.

Do not work in China on a visitor or business status where employment authorisation is required. Confirm current rules with the employer and the relevant Chinese authorities before travel.
