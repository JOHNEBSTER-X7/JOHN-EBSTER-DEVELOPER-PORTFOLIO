# Reporting catalogue

- Executive: record count and amount by domain, payment volume, fraud alerts, AML cases, reconciliation breaks, incidents and available currency codes.
- Payments: volume and amount grouped by status.
- Treasury: record count and net amount grouped separately by currency.
- Risk: fraud, AML and operations record counts grouped by status.
- Reconciliation: break count, absolute break amount and item detail.
- Data quality: invalid dates, unsafe integer amounts and missing references.
- Lineage: record counts and latest ingestion timestamp by source.
- Audit: recent batch ingestion events.
- Exports: canonical records as JSON or CSV.

These are illustrative operational MI views. They are not PRA, FCA, Bank of England, FINRA, SEC, Basel or other regulator-prescribed returns.
