# Interview guide

Be ready to explain:
1. Why monetary values use integer minor units and why currency aggregates are isolated.
2. How idempotent batch ingestion works and what key scope means.
3. Why source lineage and ingestion audit are separate from business records.
4. Which reports are simple aggregations and which need domain-specific semantics.
5. How the prototype could evolve to object storage, a warehouse engine, CDC, orchestration, RBAC and governed semantic models.
6. Why an illustrative MI report must not be represented as an official regulatory return.

A credible production roadmap would include data contracts, durable staging, quality gates, reconciled curated models, access policies, retention, monitoring, recovery objectives and independently approved regulatory definitions.
