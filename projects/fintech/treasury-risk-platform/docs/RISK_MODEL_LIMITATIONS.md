# Risk model limitations

- FX conversion rates are static examples, not market quotes.
- Stress shocks are deterministic and independently applied; correlations and nonlinear effects are excluded.
- Counterparty limits and exposures are seeded examples.
- No credit rating, probability of default, loss given default or expected credit loss model exists.
- Liquidity forecast uses deterministic dated flows; no uncertainty or intraday cash ladder.
- No VaR, expected shortfall, hedge accounting, collateral netting or regulatory capital calculations.
- Results are educational and must not inform actual funding, trading, investment or treasury decisions.
