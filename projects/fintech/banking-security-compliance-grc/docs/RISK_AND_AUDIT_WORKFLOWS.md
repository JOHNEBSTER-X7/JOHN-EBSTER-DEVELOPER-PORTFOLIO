# Risk, evidence and audit workflows

## Risk
Likelihood and impact use a 1–5 scale; inherent score is their product. Severity bands are illustrative: low below 6, medium 6–11, high 12–19 and critical 20–25. Residual score is separately recorded and must be between 1 and 25. The current model does not calculate residual score from control effectiveness.

## Evidence
Evidence records store a reference string, evidence type, reporting period, submitter and reviewer decision. They do not upload or retain the referenced file. Only submitted evidence can be reviewed once.

## Audit
Audits move from planned to in progress to completed. Findings are linked to an audit and optionally a control. Findings can be open, in progress, remediated or risk accepted. A production system should require independent approvals, immutable evidence retention, due-date escalation and closure verification.
