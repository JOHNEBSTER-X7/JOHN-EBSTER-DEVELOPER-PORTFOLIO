# Investigator playbook

1. Open the alert and review transaction details, score band and every signal explanation.
2. Check the customer's known activity and the chronology of prior transactions.
3. Review the counterparty network indicator; treat shared use as a lead, not proof of coordination.
4. Validate source data quality. IP-country and device identifiers may be incomplete or inaccurate.
5. Record the evidence reviewed, questions raised and rationale in case notes.
6. Assign the case to an appropriately authorised analyst; escalate when additional review is needed.
7. Select a final disposition only after human review: fraud confirmed, suspicious but unconfirmed, false positive or no action.
8. Record a clear closure rationale and follow the institution's approved escalation and reporting procedures.

Do not use the synthetic risk score alone to freeze an account, decline a customer, file a suspicious activity report or make any adverse decision. The API does not connect to a bank or regulatory reporting system.
