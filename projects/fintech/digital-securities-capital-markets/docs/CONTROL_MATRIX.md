# Capital markets control matrix

| Area | Demonstration control | Production requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Issuer onboarding | Independent verification flag | Due diligence, legal entity and authority checks |
| Investor eligibility | Reviewer gate | KYC/KYB, suitability and jurisdictional eligibility |
| Instrument master | ISIN pattern and duplicate check | Official identifier validation and terms governance |
| Primary allocation | Eligible investor and issue-capacity checks | Allocation policy, subscription funds and reconciliations |
| Order handling | Limit-price matching, no self-trade | Venue rulebook, market abuse controls, best execution |
| Settlement | Simulated custody transfer | Cash leg, DvP, CSD/custodian connectivity and finality |
| Corporate actions | Cash distribution register | Record-date snapshots, tax, payment rails and reconciliation |
| Audit | In-memory event history | Durable tamper-evident logs and retention |
| Security | No identity layer | SSO, MFA, RBAC, segregation of duties, encryption |
